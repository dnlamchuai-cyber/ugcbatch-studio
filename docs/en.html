04FAQ

Frequently asked

Is it free?

The quick cut uses stock visuals, free voiceover and music — $0, no watermark. Premium AI films are billed to your own model key, with the price shown before you click.

Will AI distort my product?

No. Your product image is locked during generation; only the scene and lighting change.

Does it work for US and Vietnam sellers?

Yes. UI and voiceover come in English and Vietnamese; scripts are written in your chosen language for TikTok Shop, Shopee, Amazon and Shopify.

I've never edited a video — can I use it?

Yes. Drop a product photo, skim the script, get the finished video. Switch to advanced mode only if you want per-shot control.

Where do I use it?

Right in your browser, nothing to install. Hit “Create videos now” to start.
